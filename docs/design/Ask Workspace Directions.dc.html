Paper vs Graphite

1a Paper is editorial: warm stock, serif answers, citations as quiet inline references, transcript set like a margin column. 1b Graphite is a tool: cool neutrals, one sans and one mono, bordered chips, tabs on the source panel, denser rhythm. 1b is the live prototype and was carried to every other screen, because its chips, tabs and mono metadata scale to the “Why this answer” panel and to dense tables. Paper's serif answers are worth keeping in mind for a reading mode later.
